Motion

This page checks the dialog and details animations (see design.md, “Motion”). Slow them down in the DevTools Animations panel (25% or 10%) to check them frame by frame. Then turn on prefers-reduced-motion: reduce in the Rendering panel: everything on this page must snap.

Dialog

Opening fades the dialog in and scales it up from 96% over 180ms, and the backdrop fades with it. Closing reverses that in 120ms in Chromium and is instant in other browsers. Close it each way: with a button, with Escape, and by reopening it while it’s still closing (it should turn around, not restart).

Modal dialog

Opened with showModal(). The page behind is inert, and the backdrop dims and blurs it.

Non-modal dialog

Opened with show(): no backdrop, and the page stays usable.

Popover dialog

A dialog with the popover attribute has no [open] while it’s showing, so this checks that it stays fully visible. It appears at once and fades out in Chromium. Click outside it or press Escape to close it.

Details

Opening grows the content to its full height and fades it in over 200ms; closing reverses it. Browsers without interpolate-size or ::details-content must snap exactly as before, with no fade. Click a summary twice quickly: the height should turn around from where it is. The paragraph after each group must move with the box and never overlap it.
