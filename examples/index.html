Examples

Whole pages of the kinds zinc is built for: reports, dashboards and forms, in plain semantic HTML with no classes. The people, companies and projects in them are invented. Add ?mode=dark, ?accent=forest or ?paper=beige to any page.

Reports

  • Inbox briefing

    An entry list sorted by urgency, key figures and a folded table.

  • Code health

    A dashboard: key figures, a short "do next" list and fact sheets.

  • Recommendations

    A long report with a folded index and many disclosures.

Boards

  • Feature triage

    A toolbar of filters, segmented choices with notes, and a searchable audit.

Forms

  • Questions

    A questionnaire of choice cards with a sticky action bar.

  • Every control

    A short form that uses each kind of input once.

  • Audit decisions

    A long form of per-item decisions with filters and a preview.
